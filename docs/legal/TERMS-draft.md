## What Rumbo can establish

Rumbo records work and its evidence. A passing check establishes only the condition that check tests. A matching hash identifies bytes; it does not establish correctness, permission or authorship. Reviewer statements and recorded decisions do not guarantee that a project is safe or complete.

Use your own judgment when reviewing results, granting access and taking consequential actions. Rumbo does not grant an agent permission to act on your behalf.
